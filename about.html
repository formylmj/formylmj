About The Birthday Girl

Lynn Margaret Julian

10.013.2004 monthly listeners

A talented and beautiful soul, known for being an incredibly gifted artist and a fiercely competitive gamer. But beyond her beautiful face and brilliant mind is a heart that embodies loyalty, kindness, and the truest form of a lover girl. She may be shy and a little reserved, but somehow, she naturally draws people toward her. Her warmth and genuine kindness make everyone around her want to know her, be her friend, and stay in her life. She is a deeply loving partner, an adorable baby, and the person who makes my world feel a little more complete. She is truly the other half that completes my story, the person who makes love songs make sense, and the woman I am endlessly grateful to love. And most importantly, she is the most beautiful woman in my world, the one I hope to grow old with, and the woman who will one day proudly be known as Mrs. Buen.
